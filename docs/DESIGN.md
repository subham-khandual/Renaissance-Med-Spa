# Design System — Renaissance Med Spa

## Creative direction
Premium, calm, clinical-luxury aesthetic. Use editorial photography, generous whitespace, refined serif headlines, warm neutral surfaces, and subtle botanical/rose-gold accents. Prioritize confidence and clarity over flashy effects. Treat the reference as inspiration; use only licensed/approved imagery and brand assets.

## Suggested palette
- Warm ivory `#F8F5F0` — page background
- Porcelain `#FFFCF8` — cards
- Deep espresso `#2B2523` — headings
- Muted taupe `#82756D` — body text
- Dusty rose `#B98283` — primary accent
- Champagne `#C7A878` — fine details
- Sage `#7C8B7A` — secondary accent
- White `#FFFFFF` — contrast sections

Check contrast; do not use champagne for small body text on ivory.

## Typography
- Headings: Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display
- Body/UI: Inter or Manrope
- Large, editorial hero type; readable 16–18px body; concise line lengths.

## Home page
1. Announcement strip for approved current offer (optional).
2. Header: logo, Services, About, Membership, Gallery, Contact, Schedule CTA.
3. Hero: confident skin/wellness message, real approved clinic imagery, CTA.
4. Physician/team trust section.
5. Services grid: consultation, injectables, skin rejuvenation, facials, laser, hair removal, other clinic-confirmed services.
6. Personalized consultation feature.
7. Membership highlight with terms link.
8. Before/after gallery (approved, consented assets only).
9. Testimonials only if authentic, permissioned, and compliant.
10. FAQ and location/contact CTA.
11. Footer with address, phone, email, booking, policies, social links.

## Service detail template
Breadcrumb → hero/title → concise overview → what to expect → candidacy determined at consultation → FAQ → booking CTA → related treatments. Avoid guaranteed results and individualized medical advice.

## Interaction
Subtle hover/fade transitions, reduced-motion support, sticky mobile booking CTA only if it does not obscure content. Clear buttons, accessible accordions, tap-to-call, map directions.

## Responsive
Mobile-first; one-column service cards on small screens, two-column tablet, three-column desktop. Avoid tiny text, layout shifts, auto-playing audio/video, and horizontal overflow.
